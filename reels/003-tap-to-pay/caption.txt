How does tap to pay work?
Your contactless payment card has no battery. A coil in the terminal creates a magnetic field that reaches just a few centimetres, a loop antenna inside the card turns it into power for the chip, and the chip answers with a one-time code for that payment. Phones go further and send a token instead of your real card number.
