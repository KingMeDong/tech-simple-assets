How does a QR code still work when it's torn?
Every QR code stores extra repair data next to your message, using Reed-Solomon error correction. On the strongest setting, up to about 30% of the data can be rebuilt, which is why codes survive scratches, dirt and even a logo in the middle. The three big corner squares are different: they are how your camera finds the code.
