Why does your phone die faster in the cold?
Inside a lithium-ion battery, ions move through a liquid to power your phone. In the cold, that liquid gets thicker, the ions slow down and the battery's resistance rises, so it can't deliver a sudden burst of power and the phone shuts down to protect itself. The charge is still there: warm it up and it comes back.
