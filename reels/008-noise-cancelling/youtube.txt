DESCRIPTION:
How do noise cancelling headphones work?
A microphone on the outside listens to the noise around you, and a chip creates the opposite sound wave, called anti-noise. When both reach your ear, they cancel each other out. It works best on steady, low sounds like an engine hum. Sudden sounds and voices are harder to copy, so some still get through.
