How does AirDrop send photos without the internet?
AirDrop uses Bluetooth Low Energy to find nearby Apple devices, then the two phones open a direct, encrypted Wi-Fi link between them. No router, no network, no mobile data. That's why AirDrop needs Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on.
