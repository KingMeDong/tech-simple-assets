# Official Instagram/Meta statements on Reels ranking, recommendations and reach-limiting rules (state as of 2026-11-22)

Legend used throughout: **[OFFICIAL]** = Instagram/Meta said it (on its own blog, help/creators pages, an executive's post, an earnings call, or a direct quote reported by press). **[OFFICIAL, via press]** = an official statement I could only read through a reputable press report. **[3P]** = third-party interpretation, vendor data or a blog claim. "(not fetched)" = the primary URL is cited by a secondary source, but I did not open it myself. Dates are publication dates unless noted.

## 1. Which ranking signals has Instagram officially named for Reels, and how has the emphasis changed from 2023 to 2026?

### Takeaway
On the record, Instagram has named these Reels signals: **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** (Mosseri, January 2025), building on the 2023 list of predicted actions (reshare, watching to the end, like, visiting the audio page). In September 2026 Mosseri described **engagement rate** as the main measure, with **originality, recency and "breaking content" (testing small creators with non-followers)** weighed on top of it. Instagram has **not** officially named "skip rate" or saves as ranking signals. Skip rate is an Insights metric (added in 2025), and several popular "algorithm update" claims have no primary source.

### Cited Findings
**2023 baseline (older, but still Instagram's most detailed written explainer; not formally withdrawn)**
- [OFFICIAL] Adam Mosseri, "Instagram Ranking Explained" (May 31, 2023). For Reels, "the most important predictions" are how likely you are to **reshare** a reel, **watch it all the way through**, **like** it, and **go to the audio page** (Instagram reads that last one as a sign you might make your own reel). Instagram also surveys people on whether a reel was worth their time or fun. — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [OFFICIAL] The same post lists the Reels signals in rough order of importance: (1) the viewer's activity (reels they liked, saved, reshared, commented on or engaged with recently); (2) the viewer's history with the poster; (3) information about the reel (audio, visuals, popularity); (4) information about the poster (popularity signals). — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)

**2024: shares and originality**
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Mosseri said post **share rates** (DM sends) had become a key driver of reach. Social Media Today, mid-2024 (exact date not verified). — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-post-share-rates-key-driver-reach/716540/)
- [OFFICIAL] April 30, 2024: Instagram changed ranking to favor original content and smaller creators. Eligible content is first shown to a small audience, and the best performers reach progressively wider audiences regardless of follower status (details under Q2 and Q3). — [Instagram Creators: Helping Creators Find New Audiences](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)

**January 2025: the three named signals (the most-cited statement)**
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Mosseri, in a January 2025 ranking video series (dated January 22, 2025 by one tracker): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are **watch time, likes and sends**. So when looking at your insights, pay close attention to **average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach**." — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/); date per [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Same series: "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." Connected means followers; unconnected means non-followers. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/)
- [3P] Mosseri named the three signals without ranking them, so blog claims that watch time is "#1" are interpretation. — [wiro.ae](https://wiro.ae/articles/instagram-ranking-signals-2026)

**Late 2025: user controls and metrics**
- [OFFICIAL] December 10, 2025: "Your Algorithm" launched on Reels. Viewers can see and edit the topics Instagram thinks they are interested in. It expanded to Explore (April 16, 2026, primary source not given by the tracker) and to the main Feed (June 10, 2026). — [Instagram blog: Reels algorithm control](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/reels-algorithm-control) (not fetched); [TechCrunch, Dec 10 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/10/instagrams-new-your-algorithm-tool-gives-you-more-control-over-the-reels-you-see/) (not fetched); [Social Media Today, Feed expansion](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-extends-your-algorithm-to-the-main-feed/822576/) (not fetched); dates per [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- [OFFICIAL] Meta developer blog (December 3, 2025): **skip rate** is "the percentage of views where viewers skip a Reel within the first 3 seconds, which can indicate how engaging the beginning of a Reel is." It was added to the Insights API. — [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2025/12/03/instragram-api-updates/)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] In Reels Insights, Instagram added a retention chart and skip rate, and skip rate replaced the older "view rate" metric. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/); [TechEdt, Nov 27 2025](https://www.techedt.com/instagram-introduces-retention-metrics-to-help-creators-improve-reels-performance)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Mosseri's New Year post (around December 31, 2025): "authenticity is fast becoming a scarce resource"; the bar is moving to "can you make something that only you could create?" — [explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/adam-mosseri-ai-negativity-going-to-get-worse-instagram-slop-labels-ranking-what-he-said-2026); [QuantoSei News, Jan 3 2026](https://news.quantosei.com/2026/01/03/instagrams-adam-mosseri-addresses-challenges-of-ai-content-chevron_right/). One tracker notes that this post announced **no ranking change**, despite "Year of Raw Content" headlines. — [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)

**2026: the current framing**
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Mosseri's four-part ranking series (September 3–24, 2026): "Beyond engagement, we also try to value **originality, recency, breaking content**." He also said "we now have **larger AI models** to do better at ranking overall." — [Parasocial Magazine, Oct 15 2026](https://www.parasocialmagazine.com/news/mosseri-names-four-instagram-ranking-principles-for-creators/); Part 2 (September 9) primary post: [Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdENi2fP59I/) (not fetched)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] In the same series Mosseri framed **engagement rate** as the primary measure: "So of all the people who saw the post, how many of them actually engaged with that post?" He told creators to track engagement rate rather than total views or likes. Ranking predicts each person's response, including whether they will watch a post or send it to a friend. — [Parasocial Magazine](https://www.parasocialmagazine.com/news/mosseri-names-four-instagram-ranking-principles-for-creators/)
- [OFFICIAL, via tracker] Meta's Q3 2026 earnings call (October 21, 2026) described Reels ranking **sequences** of Reels rather than single items. Reported as a ranking change, with no launch dates. — [Meta Q3 2026 earnings call transcript (PDF)](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/content_files/META-Q3-2026-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf) (not fetched), cited by [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)

**Claims with no primary source (do not treat as official)**
- [3P] A tracker flags these as unverified or invented: sends carry "3 to 5 times" the weight of likes; a "Year of Raw Content" algorithm shift (December 2025); a July 2026 update making Reels skip rate a ranking signal; original content getting "40 to 60%" more distribution; "shadowban update" claims; hashtags boosting reach; specific watch-time thresholds. — [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- [3P] A "two-way conversation" ranking signal said to date from March 2026 appears only in tool blogs and is "Not found in any Instagram source." — [dataslayer.ai](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers); see also [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- [3P] One blog says that in April 2026 Instagram added share rate, skip rate and "views over time" to Insights, and that share rate is the closest Insights metric to sends per reach. I could not confirm the April 2026 date from Instagram. — [dataslayer.ai](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers)

### Inferences
- How the emphasis moved: 2023 centered on predicted actions (reshare, completion, like, audio page). 2024 put originality and shares first. January 2025 condensed this into three per-reach ratios (watch time, likes/reach, sends/reach). 2026 adds an engagement-rate framing with originality, recency and new-creator testing on top. None of the later statements contradicts the earlier ones. They layer on each other, so the January 2025 trio is still the best set of KPIs to track.
- Skip rate is not an officially named ranking signal. Meta's own description ("how engaging the beginning of a Reel is") tracks closely with watch time and completion, which are named, so optimizing the first 3 seconds is still well grounded.
- For a no-face explainer channel, **sends/reach** is the lever Instagram itself ties most closely to reaching non-followers. A new account depends almost entirely on non-followers.

### Gaps
- I could not open Mosseri's January 2025 or September 2026 videos directly. The quotes come from Social Media Today and Parasocial Magazine.
- One secondary summary of the September 2026 series says Mosseri remarked that a brand-new account can get millions of views on its first post, and another says he suggested Instagram's systems may not yet rank AI content well. I could not tie either remark to a specific primary post.
- I did not fetch the Meta Transparency Center "Instagram Reels" AI system card (transparency.meta.com), so its current list of predictions and signals is not verified here.

## 2. How are Reels distributed to non-followers (recommendations) vs. followers, and what makes content ineligible for recommendations?

### Takeaway
Most of what people see in Reels comes from accounts they don't follow. Instagram officially describes testing eligible content on a small audience and widening it if it performs, explicitly including public posts from small creators ("breaking content", 2026). A post or a whole account can lose recommendation eligibility (non-follower reach) without being removed. Causes include the Recommendation Guidelines categories, unoriginal or repost content, watermarks and low-quality signals, repeated violations, or an unlabeled AI-generated persona. Account Status is the official place to check eligibility and appeal.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] In Reels, as in Explore, "the majority of what you see is from accounts you don't follow." — [Instagram Ranking Explained (May 31 2023)](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [OFFICIAL] Under the April 2024 ranking, eligible content is shown to a small audience first, and top performers reach progressively wider audiences, **regardless of follower status**. — [Instagram Creators: recommendations & originality (Apr 30 2024)](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] "Breaking content" (September 2026): Instagram shows public posts from small creators to people who don't follow them, testing how the content lands before wider distribution. This matters most for newer accounts. — [Parasocial Magazine](https://www.parasocialmagazine.com/news/mosseri-names-four-instagram-ranking-principles-for-creators/)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Likes weigh slightly more for followers (connected) and sends slightly more for non-followers (unconnected), January 2025. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/)
- [OFFICIAL] Content is eligible for recommendation if it is "original, does not violate our community guidelines, has no visible watermarks" and meets the Recommendation Guidelines. — [Instagram Creators (Apr 30 2024)](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- [OFFICIAL] Categories that can make content ineligible for recommendations: sensitive content (self-harm, violence, sexually suggestive material), certain health and finance content, political content, **clickbait or engagement bait**, misinformation, and **low-quality publishing**, meaning content "largely repurposed from another source." "If a post violates these guidelines, we try to avoid showing it where we recommend content." — [Instagram Creators (Apr 30 2024)](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- [OFFICIAL] Reels must follow the Recommendation Guidelines, which also apply to Explore. Violating content may stay on Instagram but is not recommended; examples include violence and regulated products such as tobacco or vaping. — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [OFFICIAL] Instagram also aims to make some reels **less visible**: low-resolution reels, watermarked reels, muted reels, reels with borders, reels that are **mostly text**, and reels already posted on Instagram. — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [OFFICIAL] Account-level effect: if an account repeatedly breaks the Recommendation Guidelines or Community Standards, "none of their posts will be eligible for recommendation for a period of time." The April 2024 page says such accounts "may be ineligible for recommendation for a period of time." — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained); [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- [OFFICIAL] **Account Status** is described as the best way to check whether an account and its content are eligible for recommendation, including eligibility to appear in Search or as a suggested account. From there you can delete affected content or appeal. Instagram said it was testing notifications that tell creators when a watermark may be limiting a reel's reach to non-followers. — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained); [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- [OFFICIAL] Content from aggregator accounts and accounts that mostly post unoriginal material is removed from recommendations, but followers still see it. "This update does not impact how we show people content from accounts they follow." — [Instagram Creators blog (Apr 30 2026)](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram); [TechCrunch (Apr 30 2026)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- [OFFICIAL] Since August 31, 2026, profiles that feature an AI-generated person and don't add the "AI-generated profile" label can become non-recommendable (details in Q4). — [Instagram Creators: AI-generated profile label](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label/)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] September 2026: **recency** weighs heavily. Newer posts get more exposure, and the Feed runs "roughly in chronological order." — [Parasocial Magazine](https://www.parasocialmagazine.com/news/mosseri-names-four-instagram-ranking-principles-for-creators/)

### Inferences
- For a brand-new channel, almost all early reach will be non-follower recommendation reach. Staying recommendation-eligible (checked in Account Status) matters more than any single optimization.
- "Clickbait or engagement bait" is an explicit ineligibility category. Hooks such as "Share this with a friend who..." or misleading titles carry policy risk, not just style risk.
- The "mostly text" demotion is directly relevant to code-generated animated graphics. Explainer Reels that are essentially animated text slides may be read as low-value. Diagram-style motion graphics plus voiceover are safer than text cards. This is an inference from the 2023 list, not an Instagram statement about animation.

### Gaps
- I did not open the full Meta Transparency Center Recommendation Guidelines page (transparency.meta.com), so the exact current wording of the "certain health and finance" and "low-quality" items is not verified.
- I found no official figure for how large the initial test audience is, or for how long a Reel keeps being tested.

## 3. Original-content rules: reposts, aggregators, watermarks, low quality, how originality is judged, and templated or AI-generated content

### Takeaway
Instagram's originality rules began for Reels on April 30, 2024 and were extended to photos and carousels on April 30, 2026. Duplicates lose out to the original, and accounts that mostly post others' work without "meaningful creative input" are removed from recommendations. Cosmetic changes (borders, watermarks, subtitles, a caption credit, speed changes) don't count. New voiceover, commentary, unique on-image text or creative graphics that add information do. Visible watermarks and low resolution are separately demoted. I found no official rule that specifically targets "templated" or AI-made original content on Instagram. The AI-related enforcement is about copying (for example, AI characters re-performing stolen material) and undisclosed AI personas.

### Cited Findings
**April 30, 2024 (Reels; still in force and extended in 2026)**
- [OFFICIAL] When Instagram finds "two or more identical pieces of content," it recommends only the original. Replacement applies when the original is relatively new, and matching uses audio and visual signals. Copies get a label linking to the original creator, and the original creator is notified. — [Instagram Creators: Helping Creators Find New Audiences](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- [OFFICIAL] Content is **not** replaced if it has been changed significantly, for example "materially edited into a meme or **narrated with a new voiceover**." — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- [OFFICIAL] Accounts that "repeatedly post content from other Instagram users that they didn't create or enhance in a material way" will not appear in recommendation surfaces. Publishers with licensing agreements or explicit creator permission are not affected. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality)
- [OFFICIAL] Eligibility requires "no visible watermarks." Watermarked and low-resolution reels are made "less visible." — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality); [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)

**April 30, 2026 (current rule; extends to photos and carousels)**
- [OFFICIAL] "Original content is work that you wholly created or reflects your unique perspective." This includes photos or videos you took and content you designed (for example a how-to guide or visual story). Third-party material can count as original if your edits add real value, such as **unique on-image text that gives more context, creative graphics that add new information**, or the remix feature. — [Instagram Creators blog: Rewarding original creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)
- [OFFICIAL] Not original: re-uploading "someone else's work in a photo, carousel, or reel without adding meaningful creative input... such as just adding a **border, watermark, subtitles, or a credit in the captions**." — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Instagram lists humor, social commentary, cultural references, unique text, creative edits or **voiceover** as additions that make content original. Low-effort edits such as adding a watermark or **changing a video's speed**, and screenshots of someone else's post even with the username credited, are not original. — [TechCrunch (Apr 30 2026)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- [OFFICIAL] Stated impact: "75% of recommendations in the US now come from original posts." — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)
- [OFFICIAL] Reinstatement: an ineligible account becomes eligible again when a "significant percentage" of its recently posted photos, carousels and reels are original within a rolling 30-day period. You can check and appeal in Account Status. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram). Sources conflict: one report summarizes this as "most" recent posts, and other reporting describes a threshold of 10 reposts in 30 days. The official wording is only "significant percentage." — [Tubefilter (Apr 30 2026)](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator)
- [OFFICIAL] Instagram's intellectual property policies still apply, and creators must obtain the necessary permissions. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram)
- [OFFICIAL, via tracker] The native **Reposts** feature (launched globally August 6, 2025) is the sanctioned way to share others' content. — [Meta newsroom, Aug 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/08/new-instagram-features-help-you-connect/) (not fetched)
- [OFFICIAL, via tracker] November 17, 2026: **Content protection** (detecting copies of a creator's content, with copies shown in their insights) opened to eligible Instagram creators. — [Instagram Creators: Introducing content protection](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/introducing-content-protection/) (not fetched), cited by [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)

**Originality in the September 2026 ranking series**
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Originality is one of the four principles: content from the original creator is weighted more heavily than reposts. Mosseri reportedly said that stolen stand-up material delivered by AI characters was a top complaint in his DMs, and called originality "the principled answer." — [Parasocial Magazine](https://www.parasocialmagazine.com/news/mosseri-names-four-instagram-ranking-principles-for-creators/); [bazzaal.com summary](https://www.bazzaal.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri)

### Inferences
- Under Instagram's own definitions, the channel's format (original scripts, code-generated diagrams, new voiceover) is clearly "original": wholly created, with designed graphics. The main originality risks would be (a) using third-party clips or screenshots (for example of Apple UI or product videos) without material transformation, (b) exporting with any tool watermark (CapCut free tier, AI-video tools), and (c) cross-posting the same file elsewhere in ways that create duplicates on Instagram.
- Subtitles alone don't make borrowed footage original. On original footage they are fine, and recommended for accessibility (see Q6).
- "Templated" risk: nothing official says that a consistent series template is penalized. Instagram's 2026 framing ("unique perspective", "only you could create") does suggest that near-identical Reels with swapped text could be read as low-value. That is an inference, not a rule.

### Gaps
- I found no Instagram document that explicitly addresses "templated", "repetitive" or "mass-produced AI" original content. Meta's separate Facebook "unoriginal content" crackdown (reported in 2025) was not researched here and may not apply to Instagram.
- The 2024 page doesn't say how long an aggregator stays ineligible. The 2026 page uses a rolling 30-day window with an undefined "significant percentage."
- I did not verify whether the watermark-notification test (mentioned in 2023) is now generally available.

## 4. AI content: when Meta applies or requires the "AI info" label (AI voice? AI visuals?), and whether AI labels or AI voiceovers reduce reach

### Takeaway
Meta applies "AI info" labels when it detects industry-standard AI signals (C2PA/IPTC metadata) or when the creator self-discloses. It **requires** disclosure for organic content with "photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created or altered." A realistic TTS voiceover plausibly falls under the audio clause, while stylized code-generated animation is not "photorealistic video." Meta says labeled content stays on the platform, and I found **no official statement that a post-level AI label reduces reach**. The one official AI-related reach penalty (August 31, 2026) applies to **profiles featuring an AI-generated person** that don't add the "AI-generated profile" label. It doesn't cover AI tools used in production, AI voices, or AI-generated visuals without a person.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Timeline of Meta's labeling policy: announced April 5, 2024; organic-content clarification May 10, 2024; "Made with AI" renamed **"AI info"** on July 1, 2024; September 12, 2024 update so labels reflect the extent of AI use; page header last updated October 23, 2025 (the change isn't described). — [Meta newsroom: Our approach to labeling AI-generated content](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)
- [OFFICIAL] Labels are applied to video, audio and images when Meta detects **industry-standard AI indicators** or when people **disclose** they are uploading AI-generated content. — [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)
- [OFFICIAL] Since September 2024, content only **modified or edited** by AI tools has its "AI info" label in the post menu, not prominently on the post. Content **generated** by AI still shows the label, along with whether it came from industry signals or self-disclosure. — [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)
- [OFFICIAL] Disclosure requirement (Meta, February 2024, reproduced in Meta's April 2024 SEC proxy filing): "We will require people to use this disclosure and label tool when they post organic content with a **photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio** that was digitally created or altered, and we **may apply penalties** if they fail to do so." Meta may add a more prominent label for high-risk, materially deceptive content. — [Meta DEF 14A, SEC (Apr 2024)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680124000034/meta-20240418.htm)
- [OFFICIAL] Labeled content **stays on the platforms** unless it violates other policies. Meta stopped removing content solely under its manipulated-video policy in July 2024. Demotion in Feed applies to content that fact-checkers rate **False or Altered**, not to AI-labeled content as such. The page lists no specific penalty for failing to disclose. — [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)
- [OFFICIAL] **"AI-generated profile" label (August 31, 2026)**: the renamed "AI creator" label, for profiles that "feature an AI-generated person" rather than a human. Creators who only use AI tools in their creative process don't need it. Creators who add it proactively "will not see a change to their profile's reach." Instagram will "limit the reach of profiles that feature an AI-generated person and do not add this label." Such profiles can lose recommendation eligibility, are notified when detected, can check Account Status, and can regain eligibility by adding the label or appealing. The page says nothing about AI voices or AI visuals that don't depict a person. — [Instagram Creators: AI-generated profile label](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label/)
- [3P] Some blogs say the August 2026 change reversed a May 2026 position that the label carried no distribution penalty. I did not verify the May 2026 statement. — [Virallized](https://www.virallized.com/instagram-ai-label); [The Prompt Insider](https://thepromptinsider.com/news/instagram-ai-generated-profile-label/)
- [3P] Several guides say Reels with **realistic AI voiceovers** need an AI label, reading Meta's "realistic-sounding audio" clause. — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/ai-disclosure-policy-for-instagram/); [Postora](https://www.postora.org/blog/ai-generated-content-labels-social-media-2026)
- [3P] Third-party consensus is that the post-level "AI info" label has **no published reach penalty**; "the label is not a ranking penalty; unoriginal work is." One blog suggests an indirect effect if some viewers use controls to see less AI content. I could not verify that such a control exists on Instagram. — [Virallized](https://www.virallized.com/instagram-ai-label); [Junibloom](https://www.junibloom.com/blog/instagram-ai-labels-what-triggers-them)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Mosseri reportedly said Instagram does not rank AI creators differently, while acknowledging "a lot of AI slop," and in mid-October (2025) that banning AI is not practical. — [explainx.ai](https://explainx.ai/blog/adam-mosseri-ai-negativity-going-to-get-worse-instagram-slop-labels-ranking-what-he-said-2026) (secondary; primary not located)
- [3P, unverified] A claim that deceptive AI video loses up to about 80% of reach has no primary source. — reported via [AuditSocials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/meta-ai-generated-content-label-policy-2026)

### Inferences
- For @tech.simple.explained: (a) the code-generated animated graphics are not photorealistic, so they don't trigger the mandatory-disclosure clause. (b) A natural-sounding TTS narrator is "realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created" on a literal reading. The conservative, policy-compliant choice is to toggle the AI label on uploads. Meta gives no official statement that this reduces reach, and its own policy says labeled content is kept. (c) The account doesn't need the "AI-generated profile" label unless it introduces an AI-generated presenter or avatar. If it ever does, adding the label avoids the official reach limit.
- Some TTS or AI-video tools embed C2PA metadata, which can trigger an automatic "AI info" label even without self-disclosure (Meta says it reads industry-standard indicators). Check export metadata if label behavior needs controlling.
- The bigger AI-related reach risk under official rules is **originality**: AI re-voicing of someone else's material is exactly what Mosseri called out. Original scripts avoid it.

### Gaps
- I found no official Instagram statement, data or test result on whether AI voiceovers or "AI info" labels change Reels distribution. Any reach effect is anecdotal or third-party.
- I could not open the current Instagram Help Center article on AI labels (help.instagram.com) to confirm present-day wording for audio-only synthetic content (for example, whether a non-cloned generic TTS voice counts as "realistic-sounding").
- No published penalty ladder for failing to disclose.

## 5. Trial Reels: what they are, who can use them, how they're shown, how results and graduation work, and official testing guidance

### Takeaway
Trial Reels (launched December 10, 2024) are shown to non-followers first and kept off followers' feeds and the profile grid. Creators see performance after about 24 hours and can share to everyone manually or automatically, based on views in the first 72 hours. Access was last officially widened in July 2025 to public accounts with at least 1,000 followers. A brand-new channel likely won't have the feature at launch, and I found no 2026 changes.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Launched December 10, 2024: share a reel with non-followers first "to see what performs best"; globally available to eligible creators "in the coming weeks." — [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfo/); [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- [OFFICIAL] Followers don't see trial reels in Feed or the Reels tab, and they don't appear on the profile grid or Reels tab unless shared with everyone. Some followers may still see them through DMs or on pages for the same audio, location or filter. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- [OFFICIAL] Metrics: about 24 hours after sharing, creators see views, likes, comments and shares, plus a comparison with their previous trials. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- [OFFICIAL] Graduation: share to everyone in one tap, or turn on automatic sharing "if it performs well based on views in its first 72 hours." The setting can be changed any time. Once shared, the reel appears on the grid and is distributed to followers. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- [OFFICIAL] Intended use: test whether new genres, storytelling formats or topics resonate before committing to them with followers. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] At launch only professional accounts had access (per a Meta spokesperson). Meta said it would keep improving auto-graduation. — [TechCrunch (Dec 10 2024)](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2024/12/13/instagram-launches-trial-reels-so-you-can-test-content-with-non-followers)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] July 2025: expanded to all creators with a **public account and at least 1,000 followers** (Mosseri). — [Social Media Today](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/); [BetaNews (Jul 14 2025)](https://betanews.com/2025/07/14/instagram-opens-up-trial-reels-feature-to-more-creators/)
- [3P, conflicting] One guide gives a 200-follower minimum for professional accounts, and Metricool says "private accounts with over 1,000 followers," which contradicts the public-account requirement (likely an error). — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-trial-reels/)

### Inferences
- At 0 followers the channel probably can't use Trial Reels. That matters less than it seems: a new account's regular Reels already go mostly to non-followers through the "breaking content" testing Mosseri described in 2026. Once past 1,000 followers, Trial Reels become useful for A/B testing hooks, voices or topic angles without diluting follower engagement-rate signals.
- Trial metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) line up with the officially named signals, so shares per view in trials is a reasonable proxy for sends per reach.

### Gaps
- I found no official 2026 update to Trial Reels eligibility, metrics or the 72-hour auto-share rule. I could not confirm the current Help Center eligibility text.
- I found no official statement on whether a graduated trial reel's earlier non-follower performance carries over into its follower distribution.

## 6. Official guidance on Reel length, aspect ratio and quality, audio, captions/subtitles, and best practices for Reels and new accounts

### Takeaway
Officially, Reels can be up to 3 minutes long (since January 2025), and Instagram says it is still "focused on short form." Vertical 9:16 is Meta's guidance. Low-resolution, watermarked, muted, bordered and mostly-text Reels are demoted. Instagram encourages captions for accessibility and Reels that are "high-quality, original and engaging from the first second." In October 2026 Mosseri said Instagram is testing longer horizontal video separately. The claim that Reels over 3 minutes aren't recommended to non-followers is widely repeated but unverified.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Mosseri on Threads (January 18, 2025): "We're now supporting reels up to three minutes long on Instagram. We still are focused on short form video over long form, but hopefully this gives creators a bit more room to be creative." The previous limit was 90 seconds. — [Mosseri Threads post](https://www.threads.com/@mosseri/post/DE-efFqyStv); [Renderforest](https://www.renderforest.com/blog/how-long-should-instagram-reel-be)
- [OFFICIAL, via tracker] October 26, 2026: Instagram began testing "Features", horizontal videos up to 90 minutes, with select US creators. This is separate from Reels. — [Mosseri Threads post](https://www.threads.com/@mosseri/post/De921QjmXvg) (not fetched), cited by [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- [3P, unverified] Reels longer than 3 minutes reportedly won't be recommended to non-followers. One guide cites a Help Center line I could not verify. Sources also disagree on the maximum upload length (10, 15, 20 or 60 minutes). — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm/); [MoonB](https://www.moonb.io/blog/instagram-reel-length); [Zeely](https://zeely.ai/blog/how-long-can-instagram-reels-be/); [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-reels/)
- [3P] Socialinsider's analysis of about 140,000 business Reels found that 45–60 seconds had the highest engagement rate and median views (a single vendor dataset). — [MoonB](https://www.moonb.io/blog/instagram-reel-length)
- [OFFICIAL] Quality demotions: **low-resolution**, **watermarked**, **muted**, **bordered**, **mostly text**, or previously posted reels are made less visible. Predicted completion ("watch it all the way through") is a top Reels prediction. — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [OFFICIAL] Meta's creator guidance: vertical content (9:16) "helps ensure your reels are immersive." This is Meta's Facebook creators page, but the same Reels format. — [Meta for Creators: Reels best practices](https://creators.facebook.com/reels-best-practices-tips-tricks)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Instagram's own Reels creation guide distinguishes **text overlays** (highlighting key moments) from **captions** (making all spoken audio visible), recommends captions for accessibility even when overlays are present, and says Reels should be "high-quality, original and engaging from the first second." — [Social Media Today: Instagram shares Reels creation tips](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-reels-creation-tips-in-new-guide/) (publication date not verified)
- [OFFICIAL] Audio: "information about the reel" includes its audio, and visiting the audio page is a predicted action. Muted reels are demoted. — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- [3P] Instagram reportedly said it would prioritize content with original sound or music from the Instagram music library. — [Rival IQ](https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/best-practices-instagram-reels/)
- [3P] Safe zones: keep text, logos and key elements out of the bottom ~35% (and top ~14%) of the 9:16 frame so the UI doesn't cover them. Third-party figures vary. — [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-reels-guide/); [Captions.ai](https://captions.ai/blog/how-to-make-instagram-reels)
- [3P] Recommended size is 1080×1920. In the main feed a Reel can display cropped to 4:5. — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-reels/)
- [OFFICIAL] Growing a new account: content is tested on a small audience and widened by performance regardless of follower count (April 2024). Small public creators are deliberately tested with non-followers (September 2026). Keep content original, watermark-free and within the Recommendation Guidelines. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality); [Parasocial Magazine](https://www.parasocialmagazine.com/news/mosseri-names-four-instagram-ranking-principles-for-creators/)

### Inferences
- For TTS-narrated explainers, burned-in captions that render the narration serve accessibility and sound-off viewing. On original content they are not an originality problem (the "subtitles" exclusion only concerns re-uploading others' work).
- Keep Reels well under 3 minutes. Mosseri's own framing is short-form first, and completion is a named prediction. A 30–60 second explainer fits both the official signals and the third-party data.
- Render at 1080×1920 or higher with no borders, letterboxing or tool watermarks. Never post a muted Reel: always include voiceover and/or music.

### Gaps

- I found no official Instagram guidance specific to TTS or synthetic voices, or to animated (non-camera) content.

## 7. Posting frequency, hashtags (5-per-post limit), keywords and search (Instagram SEO, Google indexing), and "sends" as a key signal

### Takeaway
Hashtags are officially capped at **5 per post or Reel** (announced December 2025). Mosseri has repeatedly said hashtags don't boost reach and mainly label what a post is about. Since July 10, 2025, public posts from professional accounts aged 18+ can be indexed by Google and other search engines by default, which makes captions searchable from the web. Sends (DM shares) are the signal Instagram most explicitly ties to non-follower reach. On frequency, Mosseri's most recent framing (September 2026) is that ranking prevents flooding and creators needn't fear penalties for posting often. He gave no recommended cadence.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, via press] December 18, 2025: Instagram "will gradually update the number of hashtags that you can include in a caption for a reel or post to five." Rationale: fewer, more targeted hashtags perform better than long generic lists and improve the experience. The cap followed roughly a year of tests, the latest at 3. Threads allows 1. — [Social Media Today (Dec 18 2025)](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-implementing-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/); [Business Today (Dec 19 2025)](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Mosseri said hashtags "don't work" to increase reach (February 2025) and don't improve visibility (May 2025), but are "a great way to let people know what a post is about and connect posts." — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-implementing-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- [OFFICIAL, via tracker] July 10, 2025: public content from professional (business and creator) accounts becomes Google-indexable by default, rolling out by region. — [Instagram Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/147542625391305) (not fetched), cited by [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- [3P] Scope: accounts aged 18+; owners can opt out under Settings > Account privacy; Stories, Highlights, private and personal accounts are excluded; captions and hashtags become web-searchable; indexing isn't instant; bio links aren't crawled. — [MediaCat UK](https://mediacat.uk/instagram-flips-the-switch-on-search-visibility/); [The Keyword](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/instagram-to-let-google-and-bing-index-public-posts-from-professional-accounts); [Creator Handbook](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/instagrams-indexing-update-brings-public-posts-to-google-search-results/)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Mosseri (2025 interview) said Instagram wants creator content to surface in Google and other search engines, and called in-app content search unsatisfactory. — reported in [Sociality.io](https://sociality.io/blog/instagram-search-features/) and [WERSM](https://wersm.com/instagram-content-will-now-be-searchable-on-google/) (secondary; primary interview not located)
- [OFFICIAL] Sends: "how likely you are to reshare" is the first-listed Reels prediction (2023). Sends per reach is one of the three named signals and "slightly more important for unconnected content" (January 2025). Share rates were called a key driver of reach (2024). In September 2026, ranking predicts whether you'll "send it to a friend." — [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained); [Social Media Today (2025)](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/); [Social Media Today (2024)](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-post-share-rates-key-driver-reach/716540/); [Parasocial Magazine](https://www.parasocialmagazine.com/news/mosseri-names-four-instagram-ranking-principles-for-creators/)

No official multiplier versus likes has been published. — [dataslayer.ai](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers); [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Posting frequency (September 2026): ranking keeps feeds from being flooded by high-volume posters (mainly businesses), so individual creators shouldn't fear penalties for posting often. No cadence was recommended. Recency is weighted heavily. — [Parasocial Magazine](https://www.parasocialmagazine.com/news/mosseri-names-four-instagram-ranking-principles-for-creators/)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Instagram passed 3 billion monthly users (September 24, 2025). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/24/instagram-now-has-3-billion-monthly-active-users-will-test-features-to-help-users-control-their-feeds/) (not fetched; via tracker)

### Inferences
- Use up to 5 specific hashtags (for example #techexplained or #airdrop) as topic labels, not as a reach tactic. Put descriptive, keyword-rich wording (for example "How does AirDrop work") in the first line of the caption and in on-screen text, for both Instagram's topic understanding and Google indexing. The account must be professional (creator/business) and public to be indexable.
- "Your Algorithm" (December 2025–June 2026) lets viewers choose topics, so clear, consistent topic signals (captions, on-screen text, hashtags) plausibly help the system match the channel to "technology" interests. This is an inference; Instagram hasn't said how topics are inferred.
- Explainer content that answers a common question ("How does AirDrop work?") lends itself to DM sends, which is the signal Instagram most explicitly ties to non-follower reach.

- I found no official statement on whether in-app search ranks Reels by caption keywords, spoken audio (auto-transcription) or on-screen text.
- I did not confirm whether the 5-hashtag cap is now fully rolled out to all accounts as of November 2026.
